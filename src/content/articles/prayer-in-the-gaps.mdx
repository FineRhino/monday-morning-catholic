## Short prayers count

St. Francis de Sales told busy people to scatter short prayers through the day, the way you'd glance at a friend across a crowded room. St. Teresa of Ávila called prayer "an intimate sharing between friends."

## Try this today

- Before you open email, say the Morning Offering.
- When a meeting ends, take one breath and say "Jesus, I trust in you."
- At the end of the day, look back for one moment of grace.
