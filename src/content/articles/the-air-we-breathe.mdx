Five hundred years ago it was almost impossible not to believe in God. Today belief is one option among many, and often a hard one to hold.

## Everyone feels the pressure

Charles Taylor calls this being "cross-pressured." Believers are haunted by doubt, and skeptics are haunted by moments of beauty and longing they can't explain away.

Newman put it plainly: "Ten thousand difficulties do not make one doubt."
